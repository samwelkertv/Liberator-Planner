Ships are drawn to one common scale, nose up, from game-data length × beam (grouped rows use the largest variant). No pad or garage measurements are published, so each dashed box is the largest ship confirmed as a good fit there, not a measured size. A ship bigger than that box is flagged red. Several ground vehicles share an identical 8.75 × 6 m game-data box (e.g. Mule, Cyclone, Ursa, STV), which looks like a size class rather than a measured hull. Liberator hull: 119 × 67 × 25 m (game data). Two garages: per the official Q&A (“each of the garages”) and the game data (garage A and B). The Q&A rates the front pad for XXS ships and ground vehicles; ships rated only for the upper pads are allowed there but marked untested.
