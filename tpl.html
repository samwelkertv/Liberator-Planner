Liberator deck planner

Add ships with + Add ship or drag them in from the list, then drag them around the drawing to see how they pack. Green fits, orange needs tricks, red overlaps.

Fit data: Pegaxsus Ship Fit List, LIVE 4.10.3, updated Nov 16, 2026. All fits are tested in game. Ship sizes: game data via api.star-citizen.wiki. Images: starcitizen.tools.

Drag ships to arrange them. Select one to turn it (R), nudge it (arrow keys), open its stats or remove it.

Liberator top-down: scorg.tools render. It is 1000 × 563 px, which matches the game-data 119 × 67 m, so it is used at true scale. Ships are drawn at the same scale from game-data length × beam (grouped rows use the largest variant). Overlapping ships are flagged red. The vehicle-bay outline on the internal view is approximate: it follows the centre channel, since the concept cutaway shows the bay beneath it. Fit ratings come from the sheet: deck-rated ships fit anywhere outside, and F ships only on the front pad.

RRR restock list

What the ships in your plan carry at full load, with your loadout changes applied. Use it to stock ammo, missiles and countermeasures, and to see the fuel and hull totals the Liberator will refill and repair.

In 4.10.3, RRR works in the internal garage through the mobiGlas landing services app, drawing on supplies in the adjacent cargo bay. RRR on the pads is planned for 5.0 (starcitizen.tools, citing patch notes and a Spectrum dev comment). The wiki doesn't say which cargo-bay supplies each refill uses.
