Enter what you keep on board to see how many full refills it covers. What the ships in your plan carry at full load, with your loadout changes applied. Use it to stock ammo, missiles and countermeasures, and to see the fuel and hull totals the Liberator will refill and repair.

In 4.10.3, RRR works in the internal garage through the mobiGlas landing services app, drawing on supplies in the adjacent cargo bay. RRR on the pads is planned for 5.0 (starcitizen.tools, citing patch notes and a Spectrum dev comment). The wiki doesn't say which cargo-bay supplies each refill uses. Add ships with + Add ship or drag them in from the list, then drag them around the drawing to see how they pack. Green fits, orange needs tricks, red overlaps.

Drag ships on the drawing to arrange them. Select one to turn it: drag the round handle above it for any angle (hold Shift for 15° steps), or use R / Shift+R; nudge it (arrow keys, Shift for 2 m) or remove it (Delete). From the Ships panel, drag a ship onto the drawing, or tap it and then tap the drawing. Use Outlines to show or hide the boxes around ships; the exported image follows the same setting. Drag a ship from one view onto the other to move it across, or use ⇄ Other view. Zoom in with two fingers, a trackpad pinch or the mouse wheel. When zoomed in, drag the background to pan, and use Reset zoom to go back.

Fit data: Pegaxsus Ship Fit List, LIVE 4.10.3, updated Nov 16, 2026. All fits are tested in game. Ship sizes: game data via api.star-citizen.wiki. Images: starcitizen.tools.

Liberator top-down: scorg.tools render. It is drawn at the wiki size of the Liberator, 122 × 68 × 24 m (game data says 119 × 67 m). The pad, ramp, bay and cargo marks are measured on the picture and scaled with it. Every ship is drawn at the same scale as its starcitizen.tools infobox size (length × width) for the variant you picked in Loadout, using the wiki length. When the picture shows that exact variant, the width follows the picture so nothing is squished: the Golem OX's side pods make it wider than the wiki's 7 m, for example. Pictures borrowed from another variant are fitted inside the wiki box. Some wiki sizes use store-page figures because the game-data boxes are padded (the M80, for example). Grouped rows default to their first variant. Internal view: the three cargo bays use their game-data sizes (192, 128 and 80 SCU grids). Their positions are approximate: the wiki puts the cargo on the starboard side of the garage, and the concept cutaway puts it mid-ship. Dashed edges mark the doors into the garage. The ramps follow the wiki: vehicles load from the rear ramp and unload over the forward ramp and landing pad. The Clipper and Arrow are drawn at their landed widths, measured from the wiki's landed and front views: Arrow 11.1 m (its wings tilt up but stay out), Clipper 16 m (its wings fold straight up beside the hull). On the internal view, ships can't go on the front pad (it's outside), but they can sit at the door just behind it. Overlapping ships are flagged red. The vehicle-bay outline on the internal view is approximate: it follows the centre channel, since the concept cutaway shows the bay beneath it. Fit ratings come from the sheet: deck-rated ships fit anywhere outside, and F ships only on the front pad.
